Attendance Salary Calculator

Enter the monthly salary, working days, and attendance totals to calculate salary deductions.

Salary per day is calculated using the entered working days, with 1 day of monthly credit. Only complete groups of 3 lates count as 1 absence. LC counts only in complete groups of 6: 6 LC count as 1 HCL (half-day absence), and 12 LC count as 1 full absence. Incomplete groups do not count. Monthly credit is applied before salary is deducted.
