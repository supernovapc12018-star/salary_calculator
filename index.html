Attendance Salary Calculator

Enter the monthly salary and attendance totals to calculate the salary after leave credit.

Calculation uses 22 official workdays and 1 day of monthly credit. Every 3 lates count as 1 absence. LC counts only in complete groups of 6: each group counts as 1 HCL (half-day absence), so 12 LC count as 1 full absence. Incomplete LC groups do not count. Credit is applied before salary is deducted.
